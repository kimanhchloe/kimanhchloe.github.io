<b>Existence and Regularity of Competitive Equilibria in Economies with Externalities</b>

### Overview

This project develops a rigorous general equilibrium framework for pure exchange economies with non-separable consumption and wealth externalities. By combining differential topology with equilibrium analysis, it establishes the global existence and generic regularity of competitive equilibria.

### Highlights

- Constructed a comprehensive equilibrium framework for multi-consumer and multi-commodity economies with externalities.
- Established global equilibrium existence through a multidimensional homotopy deformation framework.
- Applied the **Transversality Theorem** and **Regular Value Theorem** to prove generic regularity, showing that regular economies form an open full-measure subset with finitely many differentiable equilibria.

### Keywords

`General Equilibrium`
`Differential Topology`
`Homotopy`
`Externalities`

### Overview

This project investigates continuous-time optimal consumption and portfolio allocation under habit formation and asset mispricing. The objective is to derive explicit feedback investment strategies and establish rigorous mathematical verification of their optimality.

### Highlights

- Derived exact closed-form feedback control strategies under both CRRA and logarithmic utility.
- Decomposed the optimal portfolio into **myopic demand**, **mispricing hedging demand**, and **inflation hedging demand**.
- Proved the **Verification Theorem** using generalized multidimensional Itô calculus for jump-diffusion processes.
- Established global uniqueness by constructing a strictly negative-definite Hessian matrix for the Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman operator.

### Keywords

`Mathematical Finance`
`Stochastic Control`
`Portfolio Optimization`
`Habit Formation`
`HJB Equation`
`Mispricing`

### Overview

This project develops a two-period overlapping generations model incorporating lifecycle wariness through a non-smooth max-min utility specification. The research investigates the effects of behavioral preferences on capital accumulation, dynamic efficiency, and long-run economic growth.

### Highlights

- Solved a two-period household optimization problem with a non-smooth max-min objective, deriving a nonlinear piecewise capital accumulation law across three endogenous return-driven regimes.
- Constructed a global phase diagram for a logarithmic Cobb–Douglas economy, establishing steady-state existence, uniqueness, and finite-threshold projection.
- Derived parameter conditions for decentralized Golden Rule implementation and extended the framework to a Romer-type endogenous growth model.

### Keywords

`Overlapping Generations`
`Economic Growth`
`Dynamic Optimization`
`Dynamical Systems`
