# Research

My research lies at the intersection of **Mathematical Economics**, **Mathematical Finance**, **Stochastic Optimal Control**, and **General Equilibrium Theory**. I am particularly interested in applying rigorous mathematical analysis—including differential topology, stochastic calculus, and dynamical systems—to investigate equilibrium existence, portfolio optimization, and long-run economic dynamics.

---

## Existence and Regularity of Competitive Equilibria in Economies with Externalities

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Research Area** | Mathematical Economics · General Equilibrium Theory · Differential Topology |
| **Status** | Work in Progress |

### Overview

This project develops a rigorous general equilibrium framework for pure exchange economies with non-separable consumption and wealth externalities. By combining modern differential topology with equilibrium analysis, the project establishes the global existence and generic regularity of competitive equilibria.

### Selected Contributions

- Constructed a comprehensive general equilibrium framework integrating non-separable consumption and wealth externalities across multi-consumer and multi-commodity economies.

- Established the global existence of competitive equilibria using a multidimensional continuous homotopy deformation framework.

- Applied the **Transversality Theorem** and **Regular Value Theorem** to prove generic regularity, showing that regular economies form an open subset with full Lebesgue measure and possess finitely many equilibria that vary differentiably with economic parameters.

**Keywords**

`General Equilibrium` · `Differential Topology` · `Homotopy` · `Externalities`

## Investment Strategies with Habit Formation under Mispricing

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Research Area** | Mathematical Finance · Stochastic Control · Portfolio Optimization |
| **Status** | Work in Progress |

### Overview

This project investigates optimal consumption and portfolio allocation in financial markets with habit formation and asset mispricing. The objective is to derive explicit feedback investment strategies and rigorously verify their optimality using stochastic control theory.

### Selected Contributions

- Derived exact closed-form feedback control strategies under both CRRA and logarithmic utility functions.

- Decomposed the optimal portfolio into **myopic demand**, **mispricing hedging demand**, and **inflation hedging demand**.

- Proved the **Verification Theorem** using generalized multidimensional Itô calculus for jump-diffusion processes.

- Established global uniqueness by constructing a strictly negative-definite Hessian matrix for the Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman operator.

**Keywords**

`Portfolio Optimization` · `Stochastic Control` · `HJB Equation` · `Habit Formation` · `Mispricing`

## Overlapping Generations Model with Wariness

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Research Area** | Mathematical Economics · Economic Growth · Dynamical Systems |
| **Status** | Working Paper |

### Overview

This project develops a two-period overlapping generations (OLG) model incorporating lifecycle wariness through a non-smooth max-min utility specification. The research investigates how behavioral preferences influence capital accumulation, equilibrium dynamics, and long-run economic growth.

### Selected Contributions

- Formulated and solved a two-period household optimization problem with a non-smooth max-min objective function, deriving a nonlinear piecewise capital accumulation law across three endogenous return-driven regimes.

- Constructed a global phase diagram for a logarithmic Cobb–Douglas economy using qualitative dynamical systems theory to establish steady-state existence, uniqueness, and finite-threshold projection.

- Derived parameter conditions for decentralized Golden Rule implementation and extended the framework to a Romer-type endogenous growth model, demonstrating asymptotic growth saturation and finite-threshold stabilization.

**Keywords**

`Overlapping Generations` · `Dynamic Optimization` · `Economic Growth` · `Dynamical Systems`
