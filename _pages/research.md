# Research

My research lies at the intersection of **Mathematical Finance**, **Stochastic Optimal Control**, and **General Equilibrium Theory**. I am interested in developing rigorous mathematical models for dynamic optimization and equilibrium analysis under uncertainty. My work combines techniques from **stochastic calculus**, **differential topology**, and **dynamical systems** to study portfolio choice, market equilibrium, and long-run economic dynamics.

## Existence and Regularity of Competitive Equilibria in Economies with Externalities

*Joint work with
[Dr. Van-Quy Nguyen](https://sites.google.com/view/vanquy93/home?authuser=0).*

### Research Area

Mathematical Economics · General Equilibrium Theory · Differential Topology

### Status

🟡 Work in Progress

### Overview

This project develops a general equilibrium framework for pure exchange economies with non-separable consumption and wealth externalities. By combining modern differential topology with equilibrium theory, the project establishes both the global existence and generic regularity of competitive equilibria.

### Highlights

- Constructed a comprehensive equilibrium framework for multi-consumer and multi-commodity economies with externalities.
- Established the global existence of competitive equilibria through a multidimensional homotopy deformation approach.
- Applied the **Transversality Theorem** and **Regular Value Theorem** to prove generic regularity, showing that regular economies form an open full-measure subset and possess finitely many differentiable equilibria.

### Keywords

`General Equilibrium`
`Differential Topology`
`Homotopy`
`Externalities`

## Investment Strategies with Habit Formation under Mispricing

*Joint work with
[Dr. Van-Quy Nguyen](https://sites.google.com/view/vanquy93/home?authuser=0).*

### Research Area

Mathematical Finance · Stochastic Optimal Control · Portfolio Optimization

### Status

🟡 Work in Progress

### Overview

This project investigates continuous-time optimal consumption and portfolio allocation in incomplete financial markets with habit formation and asset mispricing. The objective is to derive explicit optimal feedback strategies and rigorously establish their mathematical optimality.

### Highlights

- Derived exact closed-form feedback controls under both CRRA and logarithmic utility functions.
- Decomposed optimal investment into **myopic demand**, **mispricing hedging demand**, and **inflation hedging demand**.
- Proved the **Verification Theorem** using generalized multidimensional Itô calculus for jump-diffusion processes.
- Established global uniqueness through a strictly negative-definite Hessian matrix for the Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman operator.

### Keywords

`Mathematical Finance`
`Stochastic Control`
`Portfolio Optimization`
`HJB Equation`
`Habit Formation`
`Mispricing`

## Overlapping Generations Model with Wariness

*Joint work with
[Dr. Van-Quy Nguyen](https://sites.google.com/view/vanquy93/home?authuser=0).*

### Research Area

Mathematical Economics · Economic Growth · Dynamical Systems

### Status

🟢 Working Paper

### Overview

This project develops a two-period overlapping generations (OLG) model incorporating lifecycle wariness through a non-smooth max-min utility specification. The research investigates how behavioral preferences affect capital accumulation, dynamic efficiency, and long-run economic growth.

### Highlights

- Solved a two-period household optimization problem with a non-smooth max-min objective, deriving a nonlinear piecewise capital accumulation law across three endogenous return-driven regimes.
- Constructed a global phase diagram for a logarithmic Cobb–Douglas economy, establishing steady-state existence, uniqueness, and finite-threshold projection.
- Derived parameter conditions for decentralized Golden Rule implementation and extended the model to Romer-type endogenous growth, demonstrating asymptotic growth saturation and finite-threshold stabilization.

### Keywords

`Overlapping Generations`
`Economic Growth`
`Dynamic Optimization`
`Dynamical Systems`

# Future Directions

My future research will continue to bridge **mathematical economics** and **mathematical finance**, with particular interests in

- Continuous-time portfolio optimization under market frictions;
- Stochastic differential games and mean-field interactions;
- Dynamic general equilibrium with heterogeneous agents;
- Asset pricing under behavioral and informational frictions;
- Applications of differential topology and stochastic analysis in economics and finance.
