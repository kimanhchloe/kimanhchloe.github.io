<div class="project-title">
Existence and Regularity of Competitive Equilibria in Economies with Externalities
</div>

<div class="project-author">
with
<a href="https://sites.google.com/view/vanquy93/home?authuser=0" target="_blank">
Dr. Van-Quy Nguyen
</a>
</div>

</summary>

<div class="project-content">

<h3>Overview</h3>

<p>
This project develops a rigorous general equilibrium framework for pure exchange economies with non-separable consumption and wealth externalities. By combining differential topology with equilibrium analysis, it establishes the global existence and generic regularity of competitive equilibria.
</p>

<h3>Highlights</h3>

<ul>

<li>
Constructed a comprehensive equilibrium framework for multi-consumer and multi-commodity economies with externalities.
</li>

<li>
Established global equilibrium existence through a multidimensional homotopy deformation framework.
</li>

<li>
Applied the <strong>Transversality Theorem</strong> and the <strong>Regular Value Theorem</strong> to prove generic regularity, showing that regular economies form an open full-measure subset with finitely many differentiable equilibria.
</li>

</ul>

<h3>Keywords</h3>

<p>

<span class="keyword">General Equilibrium</span>

<span class="keyword">Differential Topology</span>

<span class="keyword">Homotopy</span>

<span class="keyword">Externalities</span>

</p>

</div>

<details class="project-card">

<summary>

<div class="project-title">
Investment Strategies with Habit Formation under Mispricing
</div>

<div class="project-author">
with
<a href="https://sites.google.com/view/vanquy93/home?authuser=0" target="_blank">
Dr. Van-Quy Nguyen
</a>
</div>

</summary>

<div class="project-content">

<h3>Overview</h3>

<p>
This project investigates continuous-time optimal consumption and portfolio allocation under habit formation and asset mispricing. The objective is to derive explicit feedback investment strategies and establish rigorous mathematical verification of their optimality.
</p>

<h3>Highlights</h3>

<ul>

<li>
Derived exact closed-form feedback control strategies under both CRRA and logarithmic utility functions.
</li>

<li>
Decomposed the optimal portfolio into
<strong>myopic demand</strong>,
<strong>mispricing hedging demand</strong>,
and
<strong>inflation hedging demand</strong>.
</li>

<li>
Proved the
<strong>Verification Theorem</strong>
using generalized multidimensional Itô calculus for jump-diffusion processes.
</li>

<li>
Established global uniqueness through a strictly negative-definite Hessian matrix for the Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman operator.
</li>

</ul>

<h3>Keywords</h3>

<p>

<span class="keyword">Mathematical Finance</span>

<span class="keyword">Stochastic Control</span>

<span class="keyword">Portfolio Optimization</span>

<span class="keyword">HJB Equation</span>

<span class="keyword">Habit Formation</span>

<span class="keyword">Mispricing</span>

</p>

</div>

<details class="project-card">

<summary>

<div class="project-title">
Overlapping Generations Model with Wariness
</div>

<div class="project-author">
with
<a href="https://sites.google.com/view/vanquy93/home?authuser=0" target="_blank">
Dr. Van-Quy Nguyen
</a>
</div>

</summary>

<div class="project-content">

<h3>Overview</h3>

<p>
This project develops a two-period overlapping generations model incorporating lifecycle wariness through a non-smooth max-min utility specification. The research investigates how behavioral preferences affect capital accumulation, dynamic efficiency, and long-run economic growth.
</p>

<h3>Highlights</h3>

<ul>

<li>
Solved a two-period household optimization problem with a non-smooth max-min objective, deriving a nonlinear piecewise capital accumulation law across three endogenous return-driven regimes.
</li>

<li>
Constructed a global phase diagram for a logarithmic Cobb–Douglas economy, establishing steady-state existence, uniqueness, and finite-threshold projection.
</li>

<li>
Derived parameter conditions for decentralized Golden Rule implementation and extended the framework to a Romer-type endogenous growth model.
</li>

</ul>

<h3>Keywords</h3>

<p>

<span class="keyword">Overlapping Generations</span>

<span class="keyword">Economic Growth</span>

<span class="keyword">Dynamic Optimization</span>

<span class="keyword">Dynamical Systems</span>

</p>
