<h3>Overview</h3>

<p>
This project develops a rigorous general equilibrium framework for pure exchange economies with non-separable consumption and wealth externalities. By combining differential topology with equilibrium analysis, it establishes the global existence and generic regularity of competitive equilibria.
</p>

<h3>Highlights</h3>

<ul>
<li>Constructed a comprehensive equilibrium framework for multi-consumer and multi-commodity economies with externalities.</li>

<li>Established global equilibrium existence through a multidimensional homotopy deformation framework.</li>

<li>Applied the <strong>Transversality Theorem</strong> and the <strong>Regular Value Theorem</strong> to prove generic regularity.</li>
</ul>
